\section{Classical heuristics}\label{app:classic}

The classical heuristics behave on the benchmark in agreement with the review in Ref.~\cite{GuanXie2025} (Table~\ref{tab:classic}). Perpetual points lead to the target in Wang--Chen \#1, Wei, and Rabinovich. The Chua circuit has none: in the linear zones, the acceleration vanishes only at equilibria. They are also absent in SE3, SE11, and Wang--Chen \#2, while in NE4 and LE1 they exist but do not lead to the target. Sampling on nullclines (coordinate-wise zero-velocity sets~\cite{Godara2018CVS}) gives gains in hit frequency from 1.1 (SE11) and 2.6 (LE1) to 4.5 (Chua~I). Connecting curves~\cite{GuanXie2021CC} gain a factor of 2 in Wang--Chen \#1 and give no hits in SE3 and SE11. Continuation from the describing function finds the target in Chua~I within 10 integrations but applies only to Lur'e-type systems.
